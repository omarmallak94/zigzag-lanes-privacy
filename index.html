Zigzag Lanes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Zigzag Lanes ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is developed by Zigzag Lanes ("we", "us").

We do not run our own servers for the game, we do not ask for your name, email or contact details, and we do not sell personal information. The game uses services from Google, described below, which process some data to provide those features.

Information stored on your device

Your best scores and game settings are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes them.

Services from Google

ServiceWhat it is used forData it may process
Google AdMob Optional video ads you can watch to continue a run Advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, crash and performance diagnostics
Google Play Games Services Sign-in, leaderboards and your rank Your Play Games player ID, display name and avatar, and the scores you post
Google Play Billing Optional tips Purchase details. Payment information is handled by Google; we never see your card details

Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Leaderboards

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games display name, avatar and scores appear on public leaderboards that other players can see. You can control your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app, or play without signing in.

Ads and consent

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data about them should be removed, contact us at the address below.

Security

Data exchanged with Google's services is encrypted in transit.

Your choices and data deletion

  • You can play the whole game without signing in, without watching ads and without tipping.
  • To remove scores from leaderboards or delete your Play Games data, use the Google Play Games app or Google's Data & Privacy settings.
  • To remove data stored on your phone, uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • For any other privacy request, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
